# Platform guide

Limits change. Treat these as safe working values, not exact maximums.

| Platform | Hard limit | Shown before "more" | Links | Hashtags | Best image |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn (post) | 3,000 chars | ~210 chars (2–3 lines) | Clickable; posts with outside links may be shown to fewer people, so offer "link in first comment" | 3–6, at the end | 1080×1350 portrait or 1080×1080 |
| Facebook | 63,206 chars (aim for under 500) | ~125–250 chars | Clickable; a link alone builds a preview card | 2–5 | 1080×1350 or 1080×1080; 1200×627 for a link card |
| X / Twitter | 280 chars per post (Premium is longer, but don't rely on it) | Whole post | Any URL counts as 23 chars | 1–2, or none | 1200×675 landscape, or 1080×1080 |
| Instagram (feed) | 2,200 chars | ~125 chars | **Not clickable**: say "link in bio" | 5–15, at the end or in the first comment | 1080×1350 portrait |
| Threads | 500 chars | Whole post | Clickable | 1 topic tag | 1080×1350 |
| Bluesky | 300 graphemes | Whole post | Clickable; adding a link builds a card | 1–3 | 1200×675 or 1080×1080 |
| Mastodon | 500 chars (varies by server) | Whole post | Any URL counts as 23 chars | 2–5, CamelCase for screen readers | 1200×675 |

## Voice per platform

- **LinkedIn:** professional but personal. Founders do best with first person: the problem they saw, what they built, what's in it, and an ask (feedback, a try, a share). Use an emoji-bulleted list of 4–7 features. End with a question to invite comments.
- **Facebook:** warmer and shorter. 1–3 short paragraphs and a 3–5 item emoji list. Speak to the outcome, not the technology.
- **X:** a single punchy post, or a thread of 3–6 posts:
  1. A hook plus what it is (no link, so it stays readable everywhere).
  2–4. One feature or proof point per post. Attach the image or video to post 1 or 2.
  5. The call to action and link.
  Number the posts ("1/5") only if the thread has more than three posts.
- **Instagram:** the image carries the message, and the caption supports it. Open with the hook, then 3–5 short lines, then "Link in bio", then the hashtags.
- **Threads and Bluesky:** conversational, like X but with more room. Avoid hashtag clutter.
- **Mastodon:** a technical, open-source-friendly audience. Say if it's open source and what licence it uses, skip the hype, write CamelCase hashtags, and add alt text to images.

## Always

- Plain text: no Markdown asterisks or headings.
- Write alt text for every image (one sentence that describes what the image shows) and give it to the user with the posts.
- Hashtags: CamelCase for multi-word tags (#OpenSource), and never more than the limit above.
- Use at most one link per post, and only one call to action.
